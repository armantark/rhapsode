The settled learning path

1 · Cumulative chunks

Recall words 1–3, then 1–6, until the complete line appears.

2 · Fade the ending

Use the visible opening. Recall a larger hidden tail on each successful visit.

3 · Fade the opening

Recover the hidden start. Continue through the visible end of the line.

4 · Half-word cues

Use the first half of each word. Produce the complete line.

5 · First letters

Use only the first letter of each word. Then enter normal maintenance.

RESEARCH BOUNDARY

What the evidence supports

Successive relearning supports a correct-recall criterion plus later spaced practice. Diminishing cues support gradual cue removal when normal testing is too hard.

Sequence studies show position-specific effects for forward and backward training. They do not directly prove a best method for Homeric verse.

  • Successive relearning review
  • Retrieval practice with diminishing cues
  • Forward and backward sequence training
