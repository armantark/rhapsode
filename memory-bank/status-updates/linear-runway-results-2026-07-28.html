What a session is now

EVERY SMART SESSION — ONE PASS, FRONT TO BACK
reviewdue mastered lines and junctures, passage order (FSRS picks membership, position picks order)
ladder ×3each window line: one block — acquisition if new, else 3 guided reps whose cues advance between reps
junctureseams whose BOTH flanking lines are mastered
chainfinisher every session: recite the mastered prefix in order (full passage once everything is mastered)
  • The window is the first W unmastered lines; W is the `linear_window` setting (default 3, range 1–3). Settings copy states the purist tradeoff: W=1 takes ~50% longer per passage.
  • Mastery is the guided ladder itself — about 15 Good/Easy recalls across the five cue-fading phases. Your pace target holds: 15–22 study-minutes per mastered line in simulation, ~3x faster than one line per hour.
  • `random_start` is manual-only everywhere. Triage-rank selection and the new-unit trickle are gone; the runway decides selection, dueness only orders reviews.
  • Guided prompts rebuild at deal time, so undo restores the exact cue level; `ReviewStateRead` now exposes the real mastery flags the strip reads.
